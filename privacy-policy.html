Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

FirstESIM (“we”, “our”, or “us”) provides travel eSIM plans through our website and mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Collect

  • Customer details: information you provide, such as your name and mobile phone number.
  • Order information: selected eSIM plan, destination, order ID, order status, and information needed to process and support your purchase.
  • Payment evidence: receipt or payment confirmation images you choose to upload. Do not send payment-card passwords, PINs, or one-time security codes.
  • Device and app information: basic technical information needed to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the app. The app or its service providers may process device identifiers and notification tokens where applicable.
  • Support communications: information you include when contacting us.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to create and manage orders, verify payment receipts, deliver or provide access to eSIM QR codes and activation details, show order status, respond to support requests, send service-related notifications where enabled, prevent misuse, and maintain and improve our services.

3. Sharing of Information

We may share the minimum information needed with service providers that help us operate the service, such as eSIM connectivity suppliers, hosting and infrastructure providers, notification providers, and payment or payment-verification services. These providers process information to perform services for us or as otherwise described in their own privacy terms. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect our users, service, or legal rights.

4. Data Retention

We keep personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, manage orders and customer support, meet legal or accounting requirements, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and applicable requirements.

5. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Choices and Requests

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements. You can control notification permissions through your device settings. We may need to retain certain order or transaction records where required by law or necessary to resolve a transaction.

7. Children’s Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

8. International Processing

Because eSIM services and technology providers may operate in different countries, information may be processed or stored outside your country. Where required, we take steps intended to provide appropriate protection for such transfers.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions or privacy requests, contact FirstESIM at firstesimnet@gmail.com.
